Research

Five threads that make up my research programme, plus TooLQit — the toolkit I maintain.

Leptoquark & BSM collider phenomenology

Scalar and vector leptoquarks at the LHC, long-lived particles and displaced signatures, vector-like fermions, and extended scalar sectors.

Effective field theory & SMEFT

Gauge-invariant operator derivation from first principles, UV-to-SMEFT matching, operator correlations at colliders, and SMEFT global analyses with HL-LHC projections.

Flavour physics & rare decays

Leptoquark-mediated contributions to B → K(*)νν̄, K → πνν̄, B → D(*)τν, and (g − 2)μ, with correlated collider and flavour constraints on BSM parameter space.

Collider–cosmology interface

Right-handed neutrino phenomenology and its connections to leptogenesis, extended scalar sectors and electroweak phase transitions, and gravitational-wave signatures with LISA forecasts.

Statistical inference & ML methods

Simulation-based inference, DNN/BDT likelihood estimation, and Bayesian model comparison, applied to EFT coefficient extraction and public recasting tools.

TooLQit

An open-source toolkit for recasting published LHC exclusion limits onto new leptoquark models, so a search designed for one scenario can be reused to constrain another rather than rerun from scratch.
