About

Where I come from, what I studied, and how I ended up in leptoquark phenomenology.

I work on the collider phenomenology of physics beyond the Standard Model, with a focus on leptoquark models, effective field theory, and the interface between collider and flavour observables. My approach derives constraints that hold LHC direct searches, SMEFT operator correlations, and low-energy flavour data to the same standard at once, rather than treating each in isolation.

My doctoral work at IISER Thiruvananthapuram, under Dr. Tanumoy Mandal, built a unified recasting framework for scalar and vector leptoquarks, derived the complete gauge-invariant effective operator basis for vector leptoquarks from first principles, and explored leptoquark-mediated right-handed neutrino production, including displaced-vertex signatures. I'm also a developer of TooLQit, a public toolkit for recasting LHC leptoquark limits.

Alongside research, I've spent six semesters as a teaching assistant and have mentored several M.Sc. students through to publication. I grew up in Sriganganagar, Rajasthan, before moving through Delhi, Indore, and now Thiruvananthapuram for my studies.
